# English words the German scale does not count. Format: english = german
#
# Why this file exists
# --------------------
# The vectors come from a German Common Crawl model, and German web text carries
# a good deal of English. So the model knows "village", "cinema" and "captain",
# and until 2026-09-25 each of them held a number of its own. That put the
# English translation of a solution among its nearest neighbours (kino next to
# cinema, kapitän next to captain, telefon next to telephone), and on the day a
# player asked why the list for cowboy read so oddly, 62 of 2.295 games had at
# least one purely English word in their top 20. Kontexto is a German game, and
# a word no German would type has no place on its scale.
#
# What an entry does
# ------------------
# * ``village = dorf``: the word leaves the scale and is scored as the German
#   word, the way a plural is scored as its singular. Typed, it shows "dorf" and
#   carries the rank of "dorf". Every content word takes this form, so a player
#   who types English is answered, not refused.
# * ``from = -``: the word leaves the scale and is refused with "es ist zu
#   allgemein". Only function words take this form, because for them that
#   message is true: they are the English counterparts of stopwords_de.txt.
#
# A form that folded onto an entry (``villages`` onto ``village``) follows it to
# the German word, or is refused with it.
#
# What counts as purely English
# -----------------------------
# Found by scripts/find-english-words.py and then read line by line. A candidate
# is a word on the scale that the German Wiktionary does not list as a German
# word or a form of one, and that the English Wiktionary or English frequency
# names as English. The reading pass kept three kinds of candidate, and this
# file therefore leaves them out on purpose (examples, not the full record):
#   * loanwords German uses as its own: banking, briefing, caravan, makeup,
#     freestyle, dribbling, sponsoring, benchmark, governance, investment,
#     output, headline, goodwill, giveaway, handheld, checkbox, shortcut,
#     special, trading, mining, setting, borderline, brownie, audition,
#     franchising, factoring, clearing, clustering, extrusion, fermentation,
#     developer, gateway, venture, convenience, transmitter;
#   * names, places and brands, English or not: every first and last name,
#     every city and country, ford, sky (the broadcaster is typed as often as
#     the sky), seat (the carmaker), focus, shell, intel, continental, bounty,
#     sim (the card), jurassic, unreal;
#   * English spellings that are German words or German names as well: island
#     (Iceland), main and inn (rivers), wedding (a Berlin district), worms (a
#     city), storm, lessing, herder, luther and henning (authors and names),
#     bode, tip (the old spelling of Tipp), blend (the imperative of blenden),
#     loos, hamlet, mercury (Freddie Mercury more often than the metal).
# Ranch and farmer are German words (Duden), which is why they are not here.
#
# Held by backend/test_english_words.py: every entry's German target holds a
# number on the real scale, no entry is a solution, and no entry holds a number.
