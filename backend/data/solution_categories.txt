# The field every solution is filed under: one line per word of
# solution_pool.txt, "word = category id" (ids and names in categories.txt).
#
# How it was made (2026-10-01): scripts/propose-categories.py proposed a field
# for every solution from its own rank array (where the seed words of each
# field sit), and every one of the 2.325 lines was then read and decided by
# hand. The proposal was right for most concrete nouns and is never the verdict:
# it put "möhre" with the animals, "stern" with the plants, "wal" with nature
# and every chemical element wherever its strongest association pointed.
#
# Rules of the reading, so the next pass decides alike:
# * The sense a player meets first decides: "nagel" is the nail you hammer,
#   "rock" the skirt, "krebs" the illness, "kiwi" the fruit, "strom" the power.
# * Food is what is eaten, wherever it grows: fruit, nuts and vegetables are
#   "food", the living plant and the garden are "plants".
# * A place is filed under places even when its theme is elsewhere (bakery,
#   pharmacy, hospital), with three exceptions where the theme is the place:
#   zoo and animal shelter (animals), hotel and hostel (travel).
# * School subjects a child has in school are "school" (mathematik, erdkunde,
#   latein); the sciences as such are "science" (chemie, physik, biologie).
# * Toys and the playground belong to "sport", whose name says "Spiel".
# * Shapes, units, sides and positions are "concepts" (kreis, liter,
#   rückseite, mitte), as is every abstract noun no other field claims.
#
# "-" files a solution under no field at all. That is the case for exactly the
# words a field's own name would give away (categories.name_forms): "tier"
# cannot be shown as "Tiere", "musik" not as "Musik und Klang". "kategorie" is
# the one more, because the round's header reads "Kategorie". Such a word is
# never drawn by a category filter; every other mode keeps it.
#
# Sorted like solution_pool.txt; test_categories.py holds the file against it.
